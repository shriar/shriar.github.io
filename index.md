# MD. SHAHRIAR

Water resources engineer and researcher working on estuarine and coastal hydrodynamics, disaster resilience, and machine learning for hydrology.

I am currently a Water Resources Engineer at Technovative Solutions Limited (TVS) / Indetechs Software Ltd., contributing to EU-funded disaster resilience projects, and an M.Sc. researcher in Water Resource Engineering at BUET. My work spans Delft3D-based wave–tide and morphodynamic modelling, flood inundation analysis, and deep learning for river discharge forecasting.
